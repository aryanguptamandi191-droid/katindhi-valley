# Katindhi Valley General Store - Grocery Web Application

A clean, attractive grocery store website with direct WhatsApp ordering built for **Katindhi Valley General Store**.

## Store Details
- **Store Name:** Katindhi Valley General Store (कटिंढी वैली जनरल स्टोर)
- **Proprietor / Owner:** Hitesh Gupta
- **WhatsApp & Call:** +91 9418301840
- **Operating Hours:** 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Monday to Saturday)
- **Sunday:** Closed
- **Inventory:** All groceries related items available (Bags of Atta & Rice, Pulses, Cooking Oils, Spices, Sugar, Tea, Provisions & Dry Fruits)

---

## Customer Experience (`/`)
1. **Touch to Order on WhatsApp:** Customers can touch the mobile number (`9418301840`) anywhere or click **"Order on WhatsApp"** on any product card. This immediately opens WhatsApp chat with owner **Hitesh Gupta** with a pre-filled item order message.
2. **No Onsite Checkout Friction:** No registration, cart checkout, or forms required. Everything is direct and conversational on WhatsApp.
3. **Clean Wholesale & Retail Rates:** Product cards and the Rate Card display pure prices and pack sizes (e.g., 50kg bag, 15L tin). Distributor profit margins and sales statistics are strictly kept private.
4. **Live Store Timings Status:** Automatically checks current time and displays whether the store is open (8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Mon-Sat) or closed.
5. **Mobile Sticky Bottom Bar:** On phones, a sticky bottom bar allows 1-touch calling or WhatsApp ordering anytime.
6. **Rate Card Modal:** Customers and local shops can view and print the complete rate card.
